against it. Veto any entry by number.

| 18 | 13F holdings aggregated within a filing: shares and value summed per (cusip, share_type) before insert. | 19 | 13D/13G parsed from the SGML submission header only (subject/filer CIK + name, form type, acceptance, SEC file number, period-of-report when present). `percent_owned` stored NULL rather than scraped from ambiguous HTML cover pages; `event_time` uses the header period when present else falls back to filing date with `parse_confidence` recorded. | 20 | CUSIP → issuer resolution via OpenFIGI's free mapping API through a dedicated throttled/allowlisted client (not a widening of EdgarClient); results stored in `security_map` at confidence 0.9, source `openfigi`. | 21 | Convergence v1: only open-market insider purchases (code P, acquired) carry weight (1.0); insider sales score 0 and are shown as context, not scored. | Sales are noisy (tax, diversification, 10b5-1) and would flood the bullish signal an attacker could hide in; purchases are the cleaner conviction signal. | Sales are genuinely informative (especially clusters of them); scoring only purchases discards real information — revisit as a separate bearish signal post-demo. | Approved (Slice 3) |
| 22 | 13F holdings contribute 0 to the convergence score in this slice and are shown only as context. The "new-or-increased position" rule is quarter-over-quarter; the demo ingested one quarter, so the QoQ delta is not computable and scoring a single snapshot would fabricate conviction. The QoQ logic is implemented and dormant until ≥2 quarters exist. | Honesty rule 1: never score what the data cannot support; a one-quarter snapshot cannot establish an increase. | 13F presence carries some information even without the delta; excluding it weakens institutional coverage until the 8-quarter backfill lands. | Approved (Slice 3) |
| 23 | Liquidity floor, interim: exchange-listing (issuer present in SEC company_tickers). Below-floor clusters are computed but forced to bucket 'low' and excluded from the default dashboard feed (default min_confidence = medium). Slice 4 tightens to 90-day median dollar volume > $2M, after which sub-floor clusters do not publish at all. | Price/volume data does not exist until Slice 4; exchange-listing is the honest available proxy and still blocks the cheapest micro-cap manipulation via the feed exclusion. | Exchange-listing is a weak floor; a listed thin name can still be manipulated — mitigated by the ≥3-voice/≥2-class gate and tightened in Slice 4. | Approved (Slice 3, interim) |
| 24 | Signal definitions are version-locked mechanically: each definition row stores params + a sha256 `code_hash` of the computing module; `compute-signals` refuses to run unless the module hash matches the latest registered definition, forcing a version bump + changelog on any logic/constant change. Methodology public at /api/definitions. | "Signals never silently change meaning" (brief) made enforceable, not aspirational; calibration is the brand and a silent redefinition breaks it. | A refactor that does not change behavior still forces a version bump (hash changes); accepted as the cost of a hard guarantee — changelog can note "no behavior change." | Approved (Slice 3) |
| 25 | Backtest conventions: enter at the close of the first trading day AFTER the cluster's as_of; exit at the close of the first trading day on/after entry + horizon calendar days; horizons 30/90/180; metric = excess return vs SPY; a "hit" = positive excess over the horizon. | A single fixed benchmark and pre-declared horizons remove post-hoc cherry-picking; entry-after-as_of guarantees the signal was public before entry. | Close-to-close ignores intraday and slippage; acceptable for a disclosed, demo-grade backtest and stated on the methodology page. | Approved (Slice 4) |
| 26 | Episode dedup: consecutive daily clusters on one issuer with no gap > 14 days are one episode, entered once at its first day; calibration statistics count episodes, and both episode and raw-cluster counts are reported. | Daily recomputation would otherwise count one ongoing situation dozens of times, inflating n and the hit rate. | The 14-day gap is a judgment parameter; disclosed and adjustable, logged so it is visible. | Approved (Slice 4) |
| 27 | Price source: STOP. Stooq's free CSV endpoint is, at build time, gated behind a JavaScript proof-of-work anti-bot challenge; fetching requires circumventing it (ToS-violating scraping). We refuse. Price ingestion is deferred to a founder-selected clean-API feed (free key), labeled demo-grade; a licensed EOD feed is the first post-funding purchase. **Resolved: founder selected Tiingo; free key verified; split/dividend-adjusted EOD series used (source `tiingo:adjusted`).** | The data supply chain is the business and must be clean; circumventing an anti-bot measure violates that rule and likely the ToS. Decision #15 (Stooq) is thereby superseded by reality. | Delays the backtest until the founder provides a key; the alternative (scraping) is disqualifying, so the delay is correct. | Approved (Slice 4) — supersedes #15 |
| 28 | Look-ahead defense-in-depth: `score_cluster` drops any event with `knowable_time > as_of`, belt-and-braces atop the SQL gather filter; shipped as convergence v2. Bucket thresholds are NOT recalibrated (the demo lacks the resolved-episode sample to do so honestly); recalibration is deferred until adequate sample exists and will be its own logged version. | The PIT guarantee should hold even if the scorer is mis-fed; recalibrating on a tiny sample would be curve-fitting, the exact dishonesty the backtest exists to prevent. | v2 forces a recompute of clusters; cheap. Not recalibrating means buckets stay placeholders labeled "insufficient sample" — honest, if less flashy. | Approved (Slice 4) |
| 29 | Congressional trading stays flag-OFF for the demo. The pipeline + `ENABLE_CONGRESS` flag are built modular so it flips on the day a clean structured source (or licensed feed) exists; convergence computes identically with it off (Decision 11). | Honesty rule 1 + clean supply chain: better an honest absence than PDF-OCR guesses or an aggregator that violates terms. | Loses a distinctive source for the demo; mitigated because convergence is designed to survive its removal and the flag makes turning it on a config change. | Approved (Slice 5) |
| 30 | Short interest via FINRA's public consolidated short-interest API (api.finra.org returns CSV incl. current/previous short position, days-to-cover, ADV) through a dedicated allowlisted client. | 31 | Short interest is ingested and displayed as CONTEXT in Slice 5, but NOT yet weighted into the published convergence score. Honesty rule + the versioning guarantee. | Short interest does not influence the demo's headline signal yet; mitigated because it is shown as corroborating context and the weighting is one logged version bump away. | Approved (Slice 5) |
| 32 | Screenshot ticker extraction returns **symbols only**; the image is never persisted; a code output-guard (`^[A-Z.]{1,6}$`, validated against security_map) discards anything that is not a known ticker; only the fact of an extraction is logged, never contents. (Feature Spec 5.5; the spec numbered this 20 but the log is already at 31.) | Reacting to a position (reading size/entry/P&L) is advice and is off-brand; reading price shape is off-brand; a symbols-only, image-blind design is the only one that stays on the analytics side of the line. | Users may expect the platform to "analyze" the screenshot; we deliberately refuse. | Approved (Feature Spec 5.5) |
| 33 | Base-rate statements are made in pattern language ("clusters of this type were followed by positive excess return vs SPY X% of the time at H days, across N episodes since YEAR") with a non-removable honesty qualifier, and a **base-rate integrity guard**: any rate/horizon/episode/year in the (optionally LLM-rephrased) output must match the backtest record exactly, else the deterministic template renders and the divergence is logged. Below 30 episodes it reads "insufficient historical sample". | Strict framing is less punchy than a call, but a caught overstatement kills trust, and describing a pattern's history (not a user's position) is the legal line. | 34 | TradeOS states pattern base rates but never evaluates a user's specific position; the product is identical per user for the same ticker (a watchlist is names to watch, not a portfolio to grade). | Personalization would feel premium but converts analytics into regulated advice; it stays out until counsel and an entity exist. | Personalization would improve engagement; deferred deliberately. | Approved (Feature Spec 5.5) |
| 35 | Auth is demo-grade but real: argon2id password hashing, invite-gated single-use registration, breached-password check against a local list (no external call), SHA-256-hashed session tokens in HttpOnly+Secure+SameSite=Lax cookies (24h, rotate on privilege change), per-IP+per-account login rate limit, TOTP required for admin. | 36 | The free-tier signal delay is enforced as a server-side WHERE clause (`knowable_time <= now() - interval '48 hours'`) derived from the session's tier, applied in the query layer — never a client flag. | 37 | Geofence/feature gating exists from day one as a `feature_flags` table (name, enabled, blocked_countries) + one gate function, request country from a proxy header, default unrestricted for local. | 38 | Convergence v3: real **liquidity floor** = 90-day median dollar volume > $2M, computed point-in-time; sub-floor issuers do NOT publish at all (supersedes the interim exchange-listing floor of #23). | 39 | Deep EDGAR backfill (2024-07→2026-02: 33,412 13D/G + 253,866 Form 4; 661k insider txns, 492k entity-linked) completed and daily-recomputed, growing the backtest to 16,996 clusters / 427 resolved outcomes.
